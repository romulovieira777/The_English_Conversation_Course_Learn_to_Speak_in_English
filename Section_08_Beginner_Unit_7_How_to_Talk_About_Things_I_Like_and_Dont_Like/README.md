Resumo e orientações para a Section 08 — "How to Talk About Things I Like and Don't Like".

## Materiais incluídos

## Objetivos de aprendizagem
- Aprender a falar sobre preferências e aversões usando "like", "love", "don't like", "hate".
- Praticar estruturas para explicar por que você gosta ou não gosta de algo.

## Conteúdo principal (resumo)
- Frases comuns: "I like pizza.", "I don't like spiders.", "I love swimming.".
- Perguntas: "What do you like?", "Do you like... ?".

## Dicas de estudo
1. Liste 10 coisas que você gosta e 10 que não gosta; pratique dizer frases curtas.
2. Use conectivos para explicar motivos: "because", "so".

## Atividades sugeridas
- Entrevista rápida: cada aluno pergunta "What do you like doing?" e anota respostas.
- Debate simples: escolha uma atividade e discuta por que as pessoas gostam ou não.

## Pontos gramaticais rápidos
- Uso de verbos no gerúndio após "like/love/enjoy": "I like reading.".
- Negação com "don't/doesn't" para gostos: "She doesn't like coffee.".

## English version

Summary and guidance for Section 08 — "How to Talk About Things I Like and Don't Like".

## Included materials
- `Unit_7_Summary_Sheet_Beginner.pdf` — summary sheet with vocabulary about likes and preferences.

> Path: `./Unit_7_Summary_Sheet_Beginner.pdf`

## Learning objectives
- Learn to talk about preferences and dislikes using "like", "love", "don't like", "hate".
- Practice structures to explain why you like or dislike something.

## Main content (summary)
- Common sentences: "I like pizza.", "I don't like spiders.", "I love swimming.".
- Questions: "What do you like?", "Do you like... ?".

## Study tips
1. List 10 things you like and 10 you don't like; practice saying short sentences.
2. Use connectives to explain reasons: "because", "so".

## Suggested activities
- Quick interview: each student asks "What do you like doing?" and notes answers.
- Simple debate: choose an activity and discuss why people do or don't like it.

## Quick grammar points
- Use of gerunds after "like/love/enjoy": "I like reading.".
- Negation with "don't/doesn't" for likes: "She doesn't like coffee.".
